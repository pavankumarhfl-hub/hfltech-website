People & participation

HFL Community

HFL Community is the people-and-participation layer of the wider ecosystem, connecting volunteers, students, supporters and participants around HFL initiatives.

People at the centre

Students can encounter HFL through education initiatives. Volunteers can contribute to social-impact activities. Supporters and participants can connect with awareness, learning or technology initiatives according to available opportunities.

Reported reach

HFL has reported volunteers across India, including participation from Union Territories. The Foundation has also reported more than 5 lakh people connected through its work. These are reported descriptions rather than independently audited databases.

Participation

The public site does not invent a fixed membership structure, fee or selection process where those details have not been documented. Participation should follow the opportunities and programs HFL actually makes available.
