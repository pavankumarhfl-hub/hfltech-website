Pavan Kumar BN

Also known professionally as Pavan Kumar HFL, Pavan Kumar BN is the founder of HFL Foundation and HFL Tech. His work connects community initiatives and education with a long-term interest in building software, cybersecurity capabilities and artificial intelligence.

HFL Foundation

Founded in 2020, HFL began as a people-first initiative focused on social impact and community participation.

HFL Tech is the technology-focused organisation in the wider HFL ecosystem, with interests in software, AI, automation and emerging technologies.

Building with a long-term perspective

The approach is centred on building useful systems, making technology understandable, and developing products with attention to reliability, security, documentation and human accountability. The wider ambition is to connect social initiatives and technology while allowing each organisation to maintain its own identity.
