Engineering principles

Software should be understandable before it is automated.

HFL Tech's technical direction emphasises explicit system boundaries, controlled permissions, validation and human oversight.

01
Least privilege

Give systems only the access required for the task.

02
Validation

Check important outputs and actions instead of assuming correctness.

03
Observability

Make system behaviour inspectable and traceable.

04
Human control

Keep meaningful approval boundaries around consequential actions.
