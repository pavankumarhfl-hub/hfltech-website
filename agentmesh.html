04 · CONTROL

Autonomy with a boundary.

The goal is not to remove people from important decisions. It is to make software do more while keeping the boundaries clear.

ALLOW

Low-risk work

Routine retrieval, analysis and other authorised actions can move without unnecessary interruption.

REVIEW

Important changes

Actions that matter can pause for a human decision before they affect external systems.

BLOCK

Outside the policy

Actions outside the defined boundary should not be executed simply because a model suggested them.
