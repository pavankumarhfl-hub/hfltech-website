POTENTIAL RESEARCH AREAS

Start with fundamentals, simulation and evidence.

HFL Astra is the space-technology project of HFL Space Technology Private Limited. Its long-term roadmap explores launch systems, satellites, propulsion research, mission software and space applications. Student scope depends on available supervision and may initially focus on software, open data, modelling and documented systems studies.

01 / SATELLITES

Satellite systems & payload concepts

Spacecraft subsystems, payload trade-offs, power budgets, communications and mission requirements.

02 / SOFTWARE

Mission software & visualisation

Telemetry dashboards, mission data handling, ground-station workflows and engineering visualisation.

03 / SIMULATION

Orbit, dynamics & simulation

Orbital mechanics, numerical modelling, test cases, assumptions and validation in software-only environments.

04 / DATA

Earth observation & analytics

Public satellite datasets, geospatial analysis, change detection and communicating uncertainty.

05 / SYSTEMS

Systems engineering

Requirements, interfaces, verification matrices, risk registers, technical writing and design reviews.

06 / RESEARCH

Launch and propulsion studies

Supervised literature reviews and high-level system studies; no unsupervised hazardous hardware work is implied.

Relevant study areas

Aerospace, mechanical, electrical, electronics, communication, CSE, AI/ML, physics, applied mathematics and geospatial analysis.

What to include

CV, degree/year, topic preference, availability, GitHub/reports/simulations and a short statement of what you want to learn.
