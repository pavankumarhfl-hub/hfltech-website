APPLICATION GUIDE

HFL Careers: common questions.

Clear application information for people exploring HFL Tech and HFL Astra role areas.

Are all the role areas listed here confirmed vacancies?

No. The page describes role families and routes for an application enquiry. The team will confirm whether a specific position is open and share role-specific terms.

How do I apply for HFL Tech careers?

Use the official HFL Tech contact route. Include the role area, a concise introduction, your CV and relevant portfolio or GitHub links.

Does HFL Astra have a separate careers page?

Yes. HFL Astra Careers covers space-technology role areas. HFL Astra is the space-technology project of HFL Space Technology Private Limited.

Is an application fee required?

No. Do not pay money for an application or send passwords, authentication codes or private credentials. Verify unexpected requests through an official HFL contact route.
