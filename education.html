Education is a core HFL initiative focused on students, schools, learning support and technology exposure.

About this initiative

Reported program figures include more than 500 students supported each year, engagement with more than 15 government schools and work across more than 10 underserved or slum areas. HFL has stated a goal of expanding engagement to 200 government schools by 2030.
