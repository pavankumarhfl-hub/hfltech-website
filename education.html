Education

HFL Education Trust

The education initiative focuses on student support, government-school engagement, learning opportunities, underserved communities and technology exposure.

Reported activity

HFL has reported supporting more than 500 students each year, engaging with more than 15 government schools and working across more than 10 underserved or slum areas. These figures are HFL-reported figures and are not independently verified by this website.

Technology exposure

Technology exposure is part of the broader education direction. It can complement learning by helping students encounter software, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and digital tools without making every education activity a technology program.

Long-term direction

HFL has stated a goal of expanding engagement to 200 government schools by 2030. This is a future goal, not a claim that 200 schools have already been reached.

Verification note: Student, school and community figures are reported by HFL and are not independently audited by this website.
