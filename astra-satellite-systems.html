HFL ASTRA / SATELLITE SYSTEMS

Observe Earth. Connect the world.

A satellite is a system of systems. The spacecraft carries its payload; power and thermal systems keep it working; communications connect it to the ground; and the ground segment turns its observations into useful data. Each part must work together in a demanding environment.

← Back to HFL Astra

The first story is Earth observation. Instruments measure features of land, oceans, weather and atmosphere. With well-documented public datasets, learners and engineers can study changes over time, map events and build tools that make the data easier to understand.

The second story is connectivity and navigation. Communications satellites relay information over long distances, while navigation constellations broadcast timing and positioning signals. Their missions depend on precise orbits, dependable radios, accurate clocks and carefully planned ground infrastructure.

A practical starting point is software: explore public satellite imagery, visualise orbital paths, learn how payloads and downlinks work, and build small tools that explain what a satellite sees and how that information reaches people.
