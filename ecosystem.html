How the layers connect

The Foundation provides the social-impact context. Education focuses on students and schools. Community represents people and participation. Cyber Team focuses on digital safety. HFL Tech develops technology systems. These roles are distinct, but they can support one another through the wider HFL ecosystem.

People

Community participation connects volunteers, students and supporters with initiatives.

Learning

Education creates opportunities for students and schools while technology exposure connects learning with the digital world.

Technology

HFL Tech explores AI, software, security and automation, with AgentMesh and Flux AI as distinct technology directions.
