ROLE AREAS

Six disciplines. One mission.

The six disciplines below describe potential contribution areas, not confirmation of six live vacancies. Submit an expression of interest and the team will confirm whether a suitable opening is available, together with role-specific terms.

01 / AEROSPACE

Aerospace Engineering

Concept design, aerospace systems, mission planning, requirements analysis and engineering research.

02 / PROPULSION

Propulsion & Systems

Early-stage research, systems concepts, performance modelling and technical documentation.

03 / COMMUNICATIONS

Communication Systems

Satellite communications, RF fundamentals, link concepts and communications research.

04 / GNC

Guidance, Navigation & Control

Trajectory analysis, dynamics, control systems, modelling and simulation.

05 / SOFTWARE

Software & Simulation

Mission software, engineering tools, modelling, analysis and simulation workflows.

06 / RESEARCH

Research & Documentation

Technical literature review, research synthesis, reports and engineering documentation.

Who can apply

  • Students, researchers and professionals from relevant fields
  • Freshers with strong fundamentals and research interest
  • Candidates interested in space, aerospace, robotics, software or related disciplines

What to include

  • Updated CV or résumé
  • Preferred role and a brief statement of interest
  • Relevant projects, portfolio, GitHub or research samples
HOW TO APPLY

Questions about space-technology roles.

Is HFL Astra the company name?

No. HFL Astra is the space-technology project. HFL Space Technology Private Limited is the legal company name associated with the project. Read the project overview.

Are all six role areas current vacancies?

No. The list describes technical disciplines that may contribute to the roadmap. A specific opening, selection process and engagement terms are confirmed individually.

What should I include in an application enquiry?

Include your CV, preferred role area, a concise note about your interest, and links to relevant technical projects, GitHub work or research samples. Apply through the official contact route.
