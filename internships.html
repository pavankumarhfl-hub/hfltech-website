HFL TECH · STUDENT OPPORTUNITIES

Build real skills.
Work on real problems.

An online, project-oriented internship pathway intended for third- and fourth-year engineering students who want practical experience across software, AI, information systems and cybersecurity.

Express interest ↗
ELIGIBILITY

For third- and fourth-year engineering students.

The online programme is intended for students in CSE, AI/ML, Information Science & Engineering, and Cybersecurity. Applicants should be ready to learn independently, communicate clearly and document their work.

01 / SOFTWARE

Computer Science & Engineering

Web and backend development, APIs, databases, software testing and engineering fundamentals.

02 / AI & ML

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

AI application development, data preparation, model evaluation and responsible AI workflows.

03 / INFORMATION SYSTEMS

Information Science & Engineering

Data systems, automation, product engineering, information workflows and software quality.

04 / SECURITY

Cybersecurity

Defensive security, secure coding, threat modelling, documentation and authorised lab-based practice.

Include in your application

  • CV and course/year
  • Preferred track and learning goals
  • Availability and contact details
  • GitHub, portfolio or project links if available

Confirm before joining

  • Cohort dates and duration
  • Schedule, supervisor and deliverables
  • Selection and evaluation process
  • Any stipend or certificate terms, in writing
