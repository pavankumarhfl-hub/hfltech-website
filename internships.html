HFL TECH / ENGINEERING INTERNSHIPS

Put engineering into practice.
Build beyond the classroom.

HFL Tech's internship pathway is designed around practical project work, technical problem-solving and clear deliverables. Students develop experience by contributing to defined work across software, AI, information systems and cybersecurity.

01 / TECHNICAL TRACKS

Choose a field. Contribute to real work.

The programme is intended for students in the later stages of an engineering degree, with a foundation in their chosen discipline and the ability to learn independently.

TRACK 01 / SOFTWARE

Software Engineering

Frontend and backend development, APIs, databases, testing, version control and software delivery practices.

TRACK 02 / AI

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

AI application development, data preparation, model evaluation, automation and responsible AI workflows.

TRACK 03 / SYSTEMS

Information Systems

Information architecture, data workflows, systems integration, process automation and product engineering.

TRACK 04 / SECURITY

Cybersecurity

Defensive security fundamentals, secure coding, threat modelling, security documentation and authorised lab-based exercises.

02 / HOW THE PATHWAY WORKS

Structured around meaningful contribution.

Each internship assignment should have a defined scope, a responsible point of contact and an outcome that can be reviewed. The exact project scope depends on team requirements and the applicant's skills.

01 / APPLICATION

Share your profile

Submit your degree, year of study, technical interests, availability and relevant work or portfolio links.

02 / REVIEW

Technical fitment

Applications are reviewed against current project needs, relevant skills and the applicant's chosen track.

03 / PROJECT WORK

Work to defined outcomes

Selected applicants receive the applicable project scope, reporting structure, schedule and deliverables through official communication.

Who should apply

  • Third- or fourth-year engineering students
  • Students from CSE, AI/ML, ISE and cybersecurity-related disciplines
  • Applicants with foundational technical knowledge
  • Students able to communicate progress and document their work

What to include

  • Updated CV and current course/year
  • Preferred technical track and relevant skills
  • GitHub, portfolio or project links, if available
  • Availability and a short note on the work you want to contribute to
03 / OFFICIAL APPLICATION CHANNEL

Bring your skills to the table.

Submit your internship expression of interest through the official HFL Tech contact channel. Clearly state “Engineering Internship” as the subject and include your degree, year, preferred track, availability and portfolio links. The team will review your profile against relevant project requirements and respond through official communication.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Application information.

Is this an online internship pathway?

The current pathway is presented as online and project-oriented. Specific working arrangements are communicated for each assignment.

Which students can apply?

The pathway is intended primarily for third- and fourth-year engineering students in the listed technical disciplines.

Are applications automatically accepted?

No. Expressions of interest are reviewed against project requirements and available supervision capacity.

Where can I submit my profile?

Use the official HFL Tech contact page linked above and select Engineering Internship as the enquiry topic.
