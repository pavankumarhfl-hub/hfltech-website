HFL Foundation — Hope For Life

HFL Foundation, also known as Hope For Life, is the social-impact foundation initiative within the wider HFL ecosystem. It was established in 2020.

Purpose

The Foundation developed around community support, humanitarian initiatives, education and efforts intended to create practical social impact. It provides the social-impact context for the wider HFL ecosystem.

Reported reach

HFL has reported more than 5 lakh people connected through the Foundation, connections with more than 50 orphan schools or orphanage homes, and more than 5,000 beneficiaries supported every month. These figures are HFL-reported figures and are not independently verified by this website.

Relationship to the ecosystem

Foundation work sits alongside Education, Community, Cyber Team and HFL Tech. The roles are distinct: social impact is the Foundation's focus, while technology is represented separately by HFL Tech.

Verification note: The impact figures on this page are reported by HFL and are not independently audited by this website.
