Also known as Pavan Kumar HFL, Pavan is the founder behind HFL Tech and HFL Foundation, connecting technology building with a long-term commitment to people, education and community.

THE PERSON

Building with purpose.

Pavan Kumar BN is an Indian founder working across software, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, product development and social-impact initiatives. His work brings together two complementary directions: building technology through HFL Tech and supporting community-focused initiatives through HFL Foundation.

His approach is founder-led and long-term: develop practical skills, turn ideas into working systems, learn through implementation, and improve products through evidence and iteration.
