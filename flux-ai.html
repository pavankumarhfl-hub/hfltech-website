HFL Tech / Initiative

A broader direction for AI systems.

Flux AI is a separate AI initiative associated with the HFL technology ecosystem. Its direction explores coding, automation, workflows, reasoning and future AI systems.

01 / Scope

Research direction, not a claim of finished capability.

Flux AI is intentionally described as a longer-term direction. The public site separates the initiative's future-oriented scope from documented technology work that already has a concrete project description.

CODING

Developer intelligence

Exploring how AI can support software creation, workflows and technical problem solving.

AUTOMATION

Intelligent workflows

Exploring systems that connect reasoning, tools, software services and operational tasks.

FUTURE

Longer-term systems

Research direction for broader AI capabilities as the technology evolves.

02 / Relationship

Flux AI and AgentMesh are related — but not the same project.

AgentMesh focuses on agent technology, orchestration and execution workflows. Flux AI represents a broader longer-term AI direction. Both sit within the wider HFL technology ecosystem.
