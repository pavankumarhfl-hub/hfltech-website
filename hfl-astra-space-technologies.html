THE BIGGER PICTURE

Build understanding before building hardware.

Space systems are not a single invention. They are a chain of disciplines: physics, materials, electronics, software, manufacturing, testing and operations. Astra’s direction is to explore that chain in a deliberate order—beginning with learning, simulation and software, then developing deeper engineering capability as the work grows.

The goal is clear: make complex space technology understandable, and build the foundations needed to work on increasingly ambitious systems over time.
