ABOUT HFL TECH

Building technology for more capable work.

HFL Tech is an India-based technology company building intelligent software systems. AgentMesh is the flagship product: a super intelligence system designed to understand work, use tools, execute tasks and help people move from intent to outcome.

WHY HFL TECH

Less switching. More getting done.

The product direction is simple: make intelligence useful beyond a chat box by giving it the context, tools and controlled execution needed to work on a real objective.

01

Understand

Bring the task, context and constraints together before work begins.

FOUNDER

Founder-led. Built for the long term.

Pavan Kumar BN founded HFL Tech with a focus on turning ambitious technology ideas into useful products. His direction spans intelligent systems, software engineering, cybersecurity and product development, with AgentMesh as the company's flagship technology direction.

The founder story is deliberately simple: build useful technology, make it accessible, and keep improving the system as the company grows.
