THE ROLE

Founder-led by design.

The founder's role at HFL Tech is to set the product direction, engineering standard and long-term technology point of view. The company is being built around real product surfaces, technical depth and systems that can be inspected and improved over time.

That direction currently centres on AgentMesh, HFL Tech's flagship AI agent platform with human approval controls. The broader technology work includes software engineering, cybersecurity, research and other intelligent-system initiatives that sit around the company without competing with the flagship product.

The objective is not to build a marketing shell around emerging technology. It is to build the company, infrastructure, product and knowledge layer required for technology that can operate in the real world.
