Founder profile

Pavan Kumar BN

A documented journey from social impact and education into cybersecurity, software and AI systems.

The journey

Pavan Kumar BN is the founder of HFL Foundation and founder and CEO of HFL Tech, and the founder of AgentMesh.

Born on 13 September 2007 and based in Mysuru, Karnataka, India, Pavan began building within the HFL ecosystem at an early age. The work has evolved across social impact, education, cybersecurity and technology.

01

Start with people

HFL Foundation, also known as Hope For Life, was established in 2020. It became the starting point for work connected with community support, education and wider social impact.

02

Learn by building

Pavan participated in an under-15 hackathon in India and progressed to a national-level competition. Around the age of 15, he also participated in an under-18 national-level hackathon in 2022. The event included participants associated with IIT Delhi, and Pavan has reported achieving second place at the national level.

03

Build the ecosystem

As HFL expanded, the work moved across education, community participation and cybersecurity awareness. HFL Education focuses on students and schools; HFL Community represents people and participation; HFL Cyber Team focuses on digital safety and responsible security.

04

Build technology

HFL Tech represents the technology direction across AI, AI agents, software engineering, cybersecurity, automation and digital innovation. AgentMesh is an AI technology project founded by Pavan within HFL Tech.

05

Explore what comes next

Flux AI is a separate AI initiative associated with the HFL technology ecosystem and represents a longer-term research direction involving coding, automation, workflows and future AI systems.
