Founder

Pavan Kumar BN

Building from first principles across social impact, education, cybersecurity and technology.

The journey

Pavan Kumar BN is the founder of HFL Foundation and founder and CEO of HFL Tech, and the founder of AgentMesh. His work has evolved from community focused initiatives into software engineering, artificial intelligence and the development of technology systems.

Born on 13 September 2007 and based in Mysuru, Karnataka, India, Pavan began building within the HFL ecosystem at an early age. The direction has remained consistent: learn through real work, build useful systems, and keep technology connected to people.

01

Start with people

HFL Foundation, also known as Hope For Life, was established in 2020. It became the starting point for work connected with community support, education and wider social impact. As the ecosystem developed, new directions emerged around students, community participation, cybersecurity and technology.

HFL Tech therefore grew from a broader mission rather than appearing as an isolated technology project. The foundation, education initiatives, community and cybersecurity work form the wider context in which the technology direction developed.

02

Learn by building

Technology and innovation became an important part of Pavan's development through competitive events and practical projects. He participated in an under 15 hackathon in India and progressed to a national level competition.

Around the age of 15, he also participated in an under 18 national level hackathon in 2022. The event included participants associated with IIT Delhi, and Pavan has reported achieving second place at the national level. These experiences reinforced an approach based on experimentation, problem solving and execution.

03

Build the ecosystem

As HFL expanded, Pavan's role moved across several connected areas. HFL Education focused on students and government school engagement. HFL Community brought together people, volunteers, students and supporters. HFL Cyber Team focused on cybersecurity awareness, digital safety and responsible security.

The public HFL ecosystem documents several figures as HFL reported information. These are presented as reported figures rather than independently audited claims, keeping the distinction between documented activity and future ambition clear.

04

Build technology

HFL Tech represents the technology direction of the ecosystem, with work across artificial intelligence, AI agents, software engineering, cybersecurity, automation and digital innovation. The engineering approach emphasizes clear architecture, controlled permissions, secure tool use, evaluation, observability and human control.

AgentMesh is an AI technology project founded by Pavan within HFL Tech. It explores AI agents, orchestration, intelligent task execution, tools, memory, automation and developer workflows. Its architecture separates the application, agent runtime, model, tools, state or memory, and evaluation layers.

05

Explore what comes next

Flux AI is a separate AI initiative associated with the HFL technology ecosystem. It represents a longer term research direction involving AI systems, coding, automation, workflows and future capabilities. AgentMesh and Flux AI are intentionally described separately: one is a defined technology project exploring agent infrastructure and workflows, while the other represents a broader future oriented direction.

Pavan's current path combines computer science education with hands on development, AI, cybersecurity, competitive technology events, internships and practical systems building.
