HFL Ecosystem / Cyber Team
Cybersecurity

HFL Cyber Team

The cybersecurity-focused layer of the HFL ecosystem, centered on awareness, digital safety, responsible security practice and technology education.

Responsible security

Security testing, investigation and technical security work should be authorized and lawful. The public cybersecurity direction emphasizes protection, awareness and accountable practice.

Reported case metrics

HFL has reported receiving more than 4 lakh cases and solving more than 3.2 lakh cases. It has also reported more than 4,643 FIRs registered in a reported one-year period and more than 300 convictions. These are HFL-reported figures; this website does not independently verify the underlying case database, definitions, jurisdictions, time periods or legal records.

Connection to HFL Tech

HFL Tech applies security principles to technology development. In AI-agent systems such as AgentMesh, those principles include authorization, least privilege, credential isolation, validation, auditability and human control.

Important: Nothing on this website authorizes unauthorized access, testing, disruption or modification of systems. Security activity must remain authorized and lawful.
