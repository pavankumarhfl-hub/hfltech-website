What makes the project under development?

“under development” refers to the project’s ambition to extend human capabilities through advanced intelligent robotics. The project is under development, and capabilities should be evaluated through published technical evidence.

How AgentMesh powers the robotics project

AgentMesh provides the AI agent layer that connects context, planning, tools, execution, evaluation, safety, and human approval. The robotics project aims to connect this software layer with physical perception and action; integration and capabilities remain under development.

What is being developed?

Perception and context

Environmental understanding and relevant working context.

Planning and decision support

Multi-step plans, constraints, and decision support.

Authorised tools and physical action

Controlled tool access and robotics action interfaces as they are developed.

Human-robot interaction

Interaction patterns designed around explicit human oversight.

Safety and approval boundaries

Defined boundaries and review for actions with meaningful impact.

Evaluation and activity reporting

Testing, failure analysis, and evidence intended to make progress inspectable.

Development status

The project is under development. HFL Tech will publish verified technical updates, demonstrations, research notes, and evidence as development progresses. No claim is made here that the robot is commercially available, fully autonomous, or operational.
