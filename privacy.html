Privacy Policy

This page describes the general privacy approach for the HFL Tech website.

Information you provide

Contact information voluntarily submitted by visitors may be used to respond to enquiries. The site should not request passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary contact channels.

Third-party services

Hosting, forms, analytics or embedded services, if enabled, may process technical information according to their own policies. Where a third-party service is used for a form or other function, its own privacy terms also apply.

Data minimization

Only information necessary for the requested interaction should be collected. Do not send API keys, passwords or other secrets through ordinary contact channels.

Contact
