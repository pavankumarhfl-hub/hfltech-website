Privacy policy.

1. Who is responsible

This draft describes the practices of HFL Tech Private Limited (“HFL Tech”, “we”, “us”). Registered office, corporate identification number (CIN), and the legally designated privacy/grievance contact must be confirmed before publication: [CONFIRM].

2. Information we may process

  • Contact enquiries: name, work email, company, topic and message.
  • Account information: name, email address, password verifier and session/security metadata, if account features are enabled.
  • Google sign-in information: basic profile identifiers returned by Google, only if Google sign-in is configured and you choose it.
  • Product prompts and run data: information you choose to submit to AgentMesh, subject to the product configuration and applicable notices.
  • Technical information: request logs, approximate timestamps, IP address for abuse prevention, browser/device details that hosting or security systems may record.

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested features, authenticate users, maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot incidents and meet legal obligations. The precise lawful basis and notices should be validated against the services actually enabled before publication.

The final provider/subprocessor list, processing locations, international transfer safeguards and applicable agreements must be verified before publication: [CONFIRM].

5. Retention and deletion

We should retain personal information only for as long as needed for the stated purpose, security, dispute handling and legal requirements. Exact retention periods, backup deletion windows and the account-deletion process must be confirmed and implemented: [CONFIRM].

6. Cookies and browser storage

The site may use session storage for account sessions and local storage for an AgentMesh conversation identifier. See the Cookie Policy. Any analytics, advertising or non-essential tracking must be disclosed before it is enabled.

7. Security and sensitive information

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the services, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, private keys or highly sensitive personal data through general contact forms or prompts unless the relevant product explicitly supports it.
